Steroid Eye Drop Taper Schedule Maker: Printable Week-by-Week Plan in English, Gujarati and Hindi

For: PG residents

Last updated: November 2026

Steroid drops after surgery or for uveitis are reduced step by step. Patients often forget the plan or stop too early. Make a dated schedule here and print it in the patient's language. The drug name stays in English so the chemist and patient can match the bottle.

Typical tapers

SituationCommon taper
Routine cataract surgeryFour to six times a day, reducing weekly over 4 to 6 weeks
Acute anterior uveitisHourly or 2-hourly at first, then reduced every few days to weekly once cells settle
After keratoplastySlow taper over months; low dose often continued long term
Steroid respondersUse loteprednol or fluorometholone; check IOP at each visit

For the post-operative timetable of several drops together, use the eye drop timetable maker.

For learning and quick checks only. Confirm any result that changes treatment against your own examination, current guidelines and local protocols.
