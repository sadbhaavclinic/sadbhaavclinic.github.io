Resident Learning Dashboard: Filter RPC Rounds by Subspecialty and Purpose

Last updated: October 2026

How to use this page: tick one or more subspecialties and one or more purposes (for example, Glaucoma + Classifications & gradings) to see every matching RPC Rounds article. Within a group, ticks widen the list; across the two groups, they narrow it. You can also mark articles as read: your progress is saved in this browser only.

    Suggested ways to use it

    • First-year residents: tick Optics & Refraction and Basic Sciences & Pharmacology, then work down the list.
    • Before a subspecialty posting: tick that subspecialty alone and read the investigations and surgical articles first.
    • Before theory exams: tick Classifications & gradings and High-yield lists, then test yourself with the Quizzes & self-test filter.
    • Before the practical or viva: tick Viva & exam prep and Investigations & imaging.

    Preparing for an exam outside India? See the OKAP, FRCOphth and ICO guide. Indian exam aspirants can start with the AIOS-OKAT guide and exam resources.
