Ophthalmic Technician Curriculum: 12 Modules, Practical Skills Logbook and Mock Tests

For: Allied eye-care staff

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • A two-year curriculum in 12 modules, matching the topics most diploma courses and recruitment exams cover.
  • Each module lists topics, practical skills to log, pages to read on this site, and a mock test.
  • Five mock tests (200 original MCQs) cover the whole curriculum.
  • Exact syllabi vary by board and university: use your own course syllabus alongside this map.

Ophthalmic technician and eye OT assistant series: full curriculum · exams in India and worldwide · career and courses · study plan · mock tests 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 · eye OT assistant guide · practical and viva

This is a study map for ophthalmic technicians, ophthalmic assistants, refractionists and eye OT assistants. Use it to plan a diploma course year by year, to revise for a recruitment exam, or to train new staff in a clinic.

M1. Ocular anatomy and physiology (Year 1)

Topics:

  • Eyelids, conjunctiva, lacrimal system
  • Cornea and sclera, anterior chamber and angle
  • Iris, ciliary body, lens and zonules
  • Vitreous, retina, macula and choroid
  • Optic nerve, chiasm and visual pathway; pupil pathways
  • Extraocular muscles and their nerves
  • Tear film, aqueous dynamics, accommodation, colour and dark adaptation

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Identify structures on a model and slit lamp
  • Draw the visual pathway and field defects

Read: Ocular anatomy in depth · Pupils made easy · Visual pathway · Test yourself: Mock test 2

M2. Microbiology, pathology and infection control (Year 1)

Topics:

  • Bacteria, viruses, fungi and Acanthamoeba in eye infections
  • Specimen collection: corneal scraping, conjunctival swab
  • Hand hygiene, standard precautions, disinfection of instruments
  • Outbreak prevention (adenovirus)

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Prepare a slide and culture plates for a corneal scraping (assist)
  • Disinfect a tonometer prism correctly

Read: Ocular microbiology · Clinic infection control checklist · Test yourself: Mock test 2

M3. Ocular pharmacology (Year 1)

Topics:

  • Routes of drug delivery to the eye
  • Mydriatics and cycloplegics, anaesthetics, dyes
  • Glaucoma drugs by class
  • Antibiotics, antivirals, antifungals, steroids, NSAIDs, lubricants
  • Systemic drugs with eye side effects
  • Drop instillation and counselling; cap colour codes

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Instil drops and teach a patient to do so
  • Record dilation times and allergies

Read: Ocular pharmacology · Eye drop cap colour codes · Medicines that affect the eyes · Test yourself: Mock test 2

M4. Geometrical, physical and physiological optics (Year 1)

Topics:

  • Light, reflection, refraction, prisms and lenses
  • Lens power, vergence, magnification
  • Aberrations; the eye as an optical system; schematic eye
  • Refractive errors and accommodation

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Neutralise spherical and cylindrical lenses by hand
  • Solve transposition and prism numericals

Read: Clinical optics made easy · Prisms in practice · Transposition tool · Test yourself: Mock test 3

M5. Visual acuity and refraction (Year 1)

Topics:

  • Snellen, LogMAR, near charts, paediatric tests
  • Pinhole, colour vision, contrast sensitivity
  • Autorefraction and lensometry
  • Retinoscopy, subjective refraction, cross cylinder, duochrome
  • Cycloplegic refraction in children
  • Prescription writing and transposition

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Record vision in adults, children and illiterate patients
  • Retinoscopy and subjective refraction under supervision (where permitted)

Read: Vision and acuity explained · Retinoscopy tips · Jackson cross cylinder · Cycloplegic refraction · Test yourself: Mock test 3

M6. Ophthalmic instruments and investigations (Year 2)

Topics:

  • Slit lamp and its illuminations; ophthalmoscopes
  • Tonometry: Goldmann, NCT, rebound, Schiotz; calibration
  • Keratometry, topography, pachymetry, specular microscopy
  • A-scan and optical biometry; IOL formulae (concept)
  • Perimetry: strategies, reliability, printouts
  • OCT, fundus photography, FFA, B-scan, ERG/VEP (assist)

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Perform biometry with repeat criteria
  • Run a reliable 24-2 field and a good-quality OCT

Read: Slit lamp technique · Tonometers compared · Biometry checklist · Perimetry tips · OCT made easy · Test yourself: Mock test 4

M7. Common eye diseases and emergencies (Year 2)

Topics:

  • Lids, conjunctiva and cornea: infections, dry eye, pterygium, keratoconus
  • Cataract and its surgery
  • Glaucoma types and acute angle closure
  • Retina: diabetic retinopathy, AMD, detachment, vascular occlusions
  • Uveitis, neuro-ophthalmic red flags
  • Injuries and chemical burns: first aid

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Recognise and escalate red flags
  • Give first aid for chemical injury

Read: Eye symptom checker · Chemical injury first aid · Glaucoma · Retina · Test yourself: Mock test 5

M8. Binocular vision, squint and children (Year 2)

Topics:

  • Binocular single vision, fusion, stereopsis
  • Phorias and tropias; cover tests; prism measurement
  • Amblyopia and its treatment
  • Paediatric vision testing and red reflex
  • Retinopathy of prematurity (awareness)

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Cover–uncover and alternate cover test
  • Stereo and Worth four-dot tests

Read: Squint and amblyopia · Squint treatment · Eye tests for children · Test yourself: Mock test 5

M9. Dispensing, contact lenses and low vision (Year 2)

Topics:

  • Spectacle lens types, materials, coatings, multifocals
  • Frame selection, PD, fitting height
  • Soft, RGP and ortho-k lenses; care and complications
  • Low vision assessment and aids

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Measure PD and fitting height
  • Teach contact lens insertion, removal and hygiene

Read: Spectacle lens guide · Contact lens care · Low vision assessment · Test yourself: Mock test 3

M10. Operation theatre technique (Year 2)

Topics:

  • OT zones, asepsis, scrubbing and gowning
  • Instrument cleaning, sterilisation and TASS prevention
  • Phaco machine and microscope set-up
  • Surgical safety checklist and IOL verification
  • OT emergencies and biomedical waste

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Set up for cataract surgery
  • Run an autoclave cycle with indicators and records

Read: Eye OT assistant guide · Autoclaves and indicators · Sutures, needles and sterilisation · Test yourself: Mock test 5

M11. Community eye health (Year 2)

Topics:

  • Causes of blindness and visual impairment in India
  • NPCBVI, vision centres and outreach camps
  • School and diabetic screening
  • Eye donation and eye banking
  • Health education

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Conduct vision screening at a camp
  • Counsel a family about eye donation

Read: Community ophthalmology · Eye donation · Test yourself: Mock test 5

M12. Communication, ethics, records and first aid (Years 1–2)

Topics:

  • Talking to patients and families; consent support
  • Confidentiality and patient photographs
  • Records, EMR and data entry
  • Basic life support and first aid
  • Professional conduct and teamwork

Practical skills for the logbook:

  • Explain a test in the patient’s language
  • Basic life support certification

Read: EMR in the eye clinic · Counselling phrasebook (English, Gujarati, Hindi) · Test yourself: Mock test 5

Internship and logbook

  • Rotate through OPD vision and refraction, investigations (biometry, fields, OCT), OT and sterilisation, and outreach camps.
  • Log each skill with the date, number done and the supervisor’s signature.
  • Aim for real numbers: for example, at least 100 visual acuity recordings, 50 refractions (assisted), 50 biometries, 30 fields and 30 OCT scans, and 20 cataract OT set-ups before you finish (adjust to your course rules).
