Dental Infrastructure

What Do We Have

Wireless Intraoral Scanner

3 Shape, Denmark

The 3Shape TRIOS 4 is a premium, wireless intraoral scanner that captures high-speed 3D digital impressions while integrating advanced fluorescence and transillumination technologies to detect surface and interproximal caries without radiation. Its hardware features instant-heat anti-fogging tips and a hybrid configuration that allows clinicians to seamlessly plug in a backup cord if the battery runs low. Operating on the AI-powered 3Shape Unite platform, it automatically filters out soft tissue and exports open file formats like STL and PLY directly to dental laboratories.

Endomotor

Dentsply Sirona, USA

An endomotor is an electronic device used during root canal therapy to power nickel-titanium (NiTi) rotary and reciprocating files with precise mechanical torque and speed. By automating the shaping process, it significantly reduces clinician hand fatigue and accelerates treatment times compared to manual hand filing. Additionally, its built-in safety features, such as automatic reverse settings, greatly minimize the risk of file separation or instrument breakage inside the narrow root canal.

Apex Locator

J Morita, Japan

An apex locator is an electronic device used to measure the exact working length of a root canal by pinpointing the apical foramen. It utilizes changing electrical resistance to deliver highly accurate readings, minimizing the need for multiple diagnostic dental radiographs. By preventing over-instrumentation or under-filling, it ensures the root canal is safely cleaned and sealed entirely to its anatomical apex.

Dentium Implant Kit

Dentium, South Korea

A Dentium implant kit provides a structured sequence of specialized surgical drills and drivers needed to precisely prepare the bone site for successful dental implant placement. It features intuitive, color-coded components tailored to specific implant lines (like SuperLine or Implantium) to streamline surgical workflows and reduce chair time. The kit ensures safe, predictable drilling depth and width control, allowing the clinician to achieve optimal initial stability for the artificial tooth root.

Physiodispensor

NSK Nakanishi Inc, Japan

A physiodispenser is a specialized, high-torque micromotor system that provides controlled speed and irrigation necessary for dental implant surgeries and oral bone procedures. It features a built-in peristaltic pump to continuously deliver sterile saline solution, preventing thermal bone damage and necrosis during high-stress drilling. With precise, programmable torque limiters, it allows clinicians to safely drill osteotomies and measure the exact initial stability when inserting an implant.

Intra Oral Camera

Waldent, India

A Waldent intraoral camera captures high-definition, real-time images of a patient's teeth and oral cavity onto a treatment monitor or computer screen. It acts as a powerful patient education tool, allowing clinicians to visually demonstrate hidden decay, fractures, or plaque buildup to drastically improve case acceptance. Additionally, these digital images streamline clinical workflow by providing clear, immediate documentation required for dental insurance claims and accurate patient records.

Composite Warmer

USA

A composite warmer heats dental composite resin material to an optimal temperature (typically around 54°C to 68°C) before it is placed in the tooth cavity. This heating process lowers the material's viscosity, making it significantly more flowable, easier to sculpt, and better able to adapt to microscopic cavity walls. Additionally, it shortens the necessary curing time and improves the overall polymerization efficiency, resulting in a stronger, longer-lasting dental restoration.

DC Portable IOPA Machine

Vatech, South Korea

A portable IOPA machine allows clinicians to take intraoral radiographs directly at the chairside, eliminating the need to move anesthetized or compromised patients to a separate X-ray room. Its lightweight, battery-operated design makes it highly mobile, rendering it indispensable for multi-chair clinics, dental camps, home visits, and emergency bedside treatments. Equipped with advanced internal radiation shielding, it ensures maximum safety for the operator while delivering rapid diagnostic imaging that speeds up root canals and implant placements.

Zumax Surgical Loupe

Zumax, PRC

Zumax surgical loupes combine premium multi-coated optics with an ergonomic, lightweight design to deliver exceptional visual clarity and edge-to-edge sharpness during delicate procedures. Featuring customizable working distances and adaptable flip-up or through-the-lens configurations, they effectively reduce neck and eye strain while maximizing diagnostic accuracy. Elevate your practice's surgical precision and daily workflow comfort by investing in a reliable, medical-grade magnification solution built for professionals.

Available Treatments

What We Provide

Restorations

A dental restoration or dental filling is a dental restorative material used to restore the function, integrity and morphology of missing tooth structure. The structural loss typically results from caries or external trauma. It is also lost intentionally during tooth preparation to improve the aesthetics or the physical integrity of the intended restorative material. Dental restoration also refers to the replacement of missing tooth structure that is supported by dental implants.

Painless Root Canal Treatment

A root canal is a treatment to repair and save a badly damaged or infected tooth. The procedure involves removing the damaged area of the tooth (the pulp), cleaning and disinfecting it, and then filling and sealing it. The common causes affecting the pulp are a cracked tooth, a deep cavity, repeated dental treatment to the tooth, or trauma. The term "root canal" comes from cleaning of the canals inside the tooth's root. Endodontic therapy, or root canal therapy, is a sequence of treatment for the infected pulp of a tooth which results in the elimination of infection and the protection of the decontaminated tooth from future microbial invasion. Root canals, and their associated pulp chamber, are the physical hollows within a tooth that are naturally inhabited by nerve tissue, blood vessels, and other cellular entities. Together, these items constitute the dental pulp. Endodontic therapy involves the removal of these structures, the subsequent shaping, cleaning, and decontamination of the hollows with small files and irrigating solutions, and the obturation (filling) of the decontaminated canals. Filling of the cleaned and decontaminated canals is done with an inert filling such as gutta-percha and typically a eugenol-based cement. Epoxy resin is employed to bind gutta-percha in some root canal procedures. Endodontics includes both primary and secondary endodontic treatments as well as periradicular surgery, which is generally used for teeth that still have potential for salvage.

Dental Implant

The primary use of dental implants is to support dental prosthetics. Modern dental implants make use of osseointegration, the biologic process where bone fuses tightly to the surface of specific materials such as titanium and some ceramics. The integration of implant and bone can support physical loads for decades without failure.

Dental Crown

A crown is a type of dental restoration which completely caps or encircles a tooth or dental implant. Crowns are often needed when a large cavity threatens the ongoing health of a tooth. They are typically bonded to the tooth using a dental cement. Crowns can be made from many materials, which are usually fabricated using indirect methods. Crowns are often used to improve the strength or appearance of teeth. While inarguably beneficial to dental health, the procedure and materials can be relatively expensive.

Dental Bridge

A bridge is a fixed dental restoration (a fixed dental prosthesis) used to replace a missing tooth (or several teeth) by joining an artificial tooth permanently to adjacent teeth or dental implants.

We understand that each smile is unique. Hence we devise an individual treatment plan for each patient depending on the size, shape, and color of their teeth, the color of their skin and eyes, and the shape of their lips and size of their jaws. This enhances their smile line, not just so it looks perfect, but also extremely natural.

Dental Scaling

Teeth cleaning (also known as prophylaxis, literally a preventive treatment of disease) is a procedure for the removal of tartar (mineralized plaque) that may develop even with careful brushing and flossing, especially in areas that are difficult to reach in routine tooth brushing. It is often done by a dental hygienist. Professional cleaning includes tooth scaling and tooth polishing, and debridement if too much tartar has accumulated. This involves the use of various instruments or devices to loosen and remove deposits from the teeth.
