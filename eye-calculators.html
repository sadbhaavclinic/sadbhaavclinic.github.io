Trusted external tools

Free tools from research groups and professional bodies that complement the calculators on this site. They open on other websites; check that the guidance they follow applies to your patients.

  • ANDI (Amblyopia Navigator Decision-Support Instrument): from the Pediatric Eye Disease Investigator Group (PEDIG). For children aged 3 to 17: gives evidence-based advice on glasses, further treatment and follow-up intervals, drawn from 147 peer-reviewed publications. Free.
  • Brien Holden Vision Institute myopia calculator: estimates myopia progression, axial elongation and the risk of myopia onset (now including time outdoors); useful for counselling parents. Free, but asks for an email to sign in. See also our myopia control guide.
  • ODSpecs: a searchable database of soft contact lens parameters, useful for finding a lens in an unusual power, cylinder or base curve.
  • Barrett Toric calculator, Kane formula and ASCRS post-refractive calculator: for IOL decisions, alongside our two-eye IOL power calculator.
