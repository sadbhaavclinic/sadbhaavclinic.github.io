Optics & Refraction Lectures

Optics is the subject most residents postpone and most examiners enjoy. The checklist below takes you from vergence and prisms to retinoscopy, cross-cylinder, contact lenses and low vision aids, in the order they build on each other. Each topic links to a short worked page, and several have calculators so you can check your arithmetic.

Running an optical in your practice? Many ophthalmologists in private practice dispense glasses. These counter guides cover what you and your optician need day to day:

  • Lens types compared, with an order checklist
  • Sunglasses, polarised and photochromic lenses, tints and night-driving glasses
  • Lens materials and coatings · Lens designs and progressives · Frame selection and fitting
  • Dispensing reference and tolerances · Non-tolerance troubleshooting · Prisms in practice
