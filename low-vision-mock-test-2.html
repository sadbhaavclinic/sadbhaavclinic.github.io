Low Vision Mock Test 2: Definitions, Assessment, Optics and Optical Aids (40 MCQs)

For: Optometrists

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • 40 original questions on definitions, assessment, optics and optical aids.
  • Answer all, then press "Check my score": correct answers turn green, wrong ones red.
  • Open "Show answer" for the explanation.
  • Revise from the course modules.

Low Vision course: Course home · M1 · M2 · M3 · M4 · M5 · M6 · M7 · M8 · M9 · M10

Practise: Mock 1 · Mock 2 · Mock 3 · Viva and OSCE · Career guide

Set a timer for 40 minutes. The option order is mixed, so read every choice.

1. Severe visual impairment (WHO) is presenting VA worse than:

Show answer

Answer: 6/60 to 3/60. Category.

2. Visual impairment in India's 2017 definitions starts at worse than:

Show answer

Answer: 6/18. Aligned to WHO.

3. The leading cause of low vision in older adults in many countries is:

Show answer

Answer: AMD. Central loss.

4. Tunnel vision is typical of:

Show answer

Answer: Advanced glaucoma or RP. Peripheral loss.

5. A patient reads the 1.0 line of a 4 m logMAR chart held at 1 m. The logMAR acuity is:

Show answer

Answer: 1.6. Add log(4/1) = 0.6 when testing at 1 m instead of 4 m.

6. Critical print size is found with:

Show answer

Answer: Continuous text reading charts measuring speed. MNREAD-type.

7. Acuity reserve suggests using print about:

Show answer

Answer: Two lines larger than threshold. Fluency.

8. Pelli-Robson measures:

Show answer

Answer: Contrast sensitivity. Log units.

9. The Amsler grid detects:

Show answer

Answer: Central distortion and scotoma. Macula.

10. Microperimetry shows:

Show answer

Answer: Fixation stability and PRL. Central sensitivity.

11. Relative distance magnification from 25 cm to 5 cm is:

Show answer

Answer: 5×. 25/5.

12. Required magnification for N40 to N10:

Show answer

Answer: 4×. 40/10.

13. Kestenbaum add for 6/24 is:

Show answer

Answer: +4 D. 24/6.

14. Working distance for +12 D:

Show answer

Answer: about 8 cm. 1/12 m.

15. Equivalent power for 5× (M = F/4):

Show answer

Answer: +20 D. 4 × 5.

16. Field of view decreases with:

Show answer

Answer: Higher magnification. Trade-off.

17. A Keplerian telescope differs from Galilean by:

Show answer

Answer: Two plus lenses with prism for upright image. Longer, better optics.

18. Reading caps on telescopes:

Show answer

Answer: Allow near focusing. Plus lens.

19. Binocular high adds need:

Show answer

Answer: Base-in prism. Convergence.

20. Monocular high adds are usual beyond about:

Show answer

Answer: +10 to +12 D. Convergence limit.

21. Hand magnifiers are held at focal distance when using:

Show answer

Answer: Distance glasses. Parallel light.

22. Stand magnifiers suit people with:

Show answer

Answer: Tremor. Rest on page.

23. Illuminated magnifiers:

Show answer

Answer: Improve task lighting. Light.

24. A person reads N12 with a +8 D lens; working distance is:

Show answer

Answer: 12.5 cm. 1/8 m.

25. Low vision refraction should use:

Show answer

Answer: Trial frame and larger steps. Sensitive changes.

26. Glare sensitivity is especially common in:

Show answer

Answer: Albinism. Filters.

27. A typical goal-based question is:

Show answer

Answer: What tasks would you like to do better?. Function.

28. Spectacle magnifiers give the:

Show answer

Answer: Widest field for a given power. Close to eye.

29. Hand-held telescopes are used for:

Show answer

Answer: Spotting distant signs. Distance.

30. Bioptics are mounted:

Show answer

Answer: High on the carrier lens. Look under.

31. Peli prisms help:

Show answer

Answer: Hemianopia. Field awareness.

32. Right hemianopia affects reading because:

Show answer

Answer: Upcoming words fall in the blind field. Reading.

33. Telescope training begins with:

Show answer

Answer: Localisation. Find target.

34. Electronic magnification is a type of:

Show answer

Answer: Projection/electronic. Screens.

35. Large print is an example of:

Show answer

Answer: Relative size magnification. Bigger object.

36. A 4× aid for N24 print gives:

Show answer

Answer: N6. 24/4.

37. Contrast sensitivity loss mainly affects:

Show answer

Answer: Faces, steps and kerbs. Real-world.

38. Binocular field testing matters for:

Show answer

Answer: Mobility and driving. Function.

39. Video magnifiers offer magnification up to about:

Show answer

Answer: 60× or more. Desktop.

40. A person with 6/60 and a central scotoma may benefit from:

Show answer

Answer: Eccentric viewing training plus magnification. Rehabilitation.
