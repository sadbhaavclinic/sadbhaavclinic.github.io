Eye Banking Module 8: Preservation and Storage Media

For: Allied eye-care staff

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • Moist chamber (whole globe): short-term, process within about 24 hours.
  • MK medium: up to about 4 days at 2–8 °C; intermediate media (Optisol-GS type, Cornisol): up to about 1–2 weeks at 2–8 °C.
  • Organ culture (Europe): 31–37 °C, up to about 4 weeks, with sterility testing.
  • Glycerol or other long-term methods preserve tissue for tectonic use only (no living endothelium).

MethodTemperatureTypical storage timeNotes

Intermediate-term media (e.g. Optisol-GS-type, Cornisol, Eusol-C)2–8 °CAbout 7–14 days (by product and standards)Chondroitin sulphate, dextran, antibiotics, buffers
Organ culture31–37 °CUp to about 4 weeksAllows sterility testing and endothelial assessment; needs deswelling medium before surgery
Glycerol preservationRoom temperature (anhydrous glycerol)MonthsTectonic grafts only
CryopreservationLiquid nitrogenLong-termSpecialised; rarely used

Storage practice

  • Refrigerators dedicated to tissue, with continuous temperature monitoring, alarms and logs; backup power.
  • Do not freeze corneas in MK or intermediate media (ice crystals destroy cells).
  • Media checked for expiry, colour change (pH indicator), turbidity (contamination).
  • Label: donor ID, eye, date and time of preservation, medium lot number and expiry, tissue expiry.
  • Warm tissue to room temperature as per protocol before surgery or specular imaging.

Sclera storage

Sclera is cleaned of uveal tissue and stored in 70–95% alcohol or glycerol, at room temperature or refrigerated, per standards; labelled and tracked like cornea.

Microbiology

  • Antibiotics in media reduce bacterial contamination; fungal contamination is an increasing concern (some banks add antifungals in trials).
  • Rim cultures at surgery may be taken; positive results reported to surgeon and bank.

Practice questions (short answer and viva)

How long can corneas be stored in MK medium?

About 4 days at 2–8 °C.

What is the advantage of organ culture?

Longer storage (weeks), sterility testing and endothelial assessment.

Why must media not be frozen?

Ice crystals destroy endothelial cells.

What does a colour change in storage medium suggest?

pH change, possibly contamination.

How is sclera stored?

In alcohol or glycerol per standards.
