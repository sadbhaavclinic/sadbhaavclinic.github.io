Ophthalmic Technician Mock Test 1 (Mixed): 40 MCQs with Answers

For: Allied eye-care staff

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • 40 original questions in the style of technician and refractionist recruitment exams.
  • Choose your answers, then press "Check my score": correct answers turn green, wrong ones red.
  • Open "Show answer" for the explanation.
  • Aim for 30 or more before your exam.

Ophthalmic technician and eye OT assistant series: full curriculum · exams in India and worldwide · career and courses · study plan · mock tests 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 · eye OT assistant guide · practical and viva

Set a timer for 40 minutes and answer every question before checking. The questions are original and cover the topics most often tested.

1. The refractive power of the cornea is about:

Show answer

Answer: +43 D. The cornea provides about two-thirds of the eye's total power of about +60 D, roughly +43 D.

2. Aqueous humour is produced by the:

Show answer

Answer: Ciliary epithelium (non-pigmented). The non-pigmented ciliary epithelium of the ciliary processes secretes aqueous.

3. The commonest cause of blindness in India is:

Show answer

Answer: Cataract. Cataract remains the leading cause of blindness in national surveys.

4. Transpose −1.00 / +2.00 × 90 into minus cylinder form:

Show answer

Answer: +1.00 / −2.00 × 180. Add the cylinder to the sphere (−1 + 2 = +1), change the cylinder sign, and rotate the axis by 90°.

5. Prentice's rule: a 4 D lens decentred by 5 mm produces:

Show answer

Answer: 2 prism D. Prism (Δ) = decentration in cm × power: 0.5 × 4 = 2Δ.

6. 6/60 vision is equivalent to:

Show answer

Answer: 20/200. 6/60 equals 20/200 (decimal 0.1).

7. The pinhole test improving vision suggests:

Show answer

Answer: Refractive error. A pinhole reduces blur from refractive error; little improvement suggests another cause.

8. The red–green duochrome test is based on:

Show answer

Answer: Chromatic aberration. Red light focuses behind green light because of chromatic aberration, so the balance shows over- or under-correction.

9. In retinoscopy at 66 cm, the working distance correction is:

Show answer

Answer: −1.50 D. 1 ÷ 0.66 m ≈ 1.50 D, subtracted from the gross retinoscopy result.

10. Goldmann applanation tonometry measures pressure by flattening a corneal area of diameter:

Show answer

Answer: 3.06 mm. At 3.06 mm, corneal rigidity and tear-film attraction roughly cancel out.

11. A thin cornea causes Goldmann IOP readings to be:

Show answer

Answer: Underestimated. Thin corneas are easier to flatten, so pressure reads falsely low.

12. In A-scan biometry, an error of 1 mm in axial length causes an IOL power error of about:

Show answer

Answer: 2.5–3 D. Roughly 2.5–3 D per mm, which is why biometry must be meticulous.

13. Biometry should be repeated if axial lengths of the two eyes differ by more than:

Show answer

Answer: 0.3 mm. A difference over 0.3 mm (without a known reason) needs re-measurement.

14. The fluorescein filter used with Goldmann tonometry is:

Show answer

Answer: Cobalt blue. Cobalt blue light makes the fluorescein mires glow.

15. In Humphrey perimetry, a fixation-loss rate above about this value makes the test unreliable:

Show answer

Answer: 20%. More than about 20% fixation losses suggests unreliable fixation.

16. The 24-2 Humphrey programme tests points within:

Show answer

Answer: 24° of fixation. 24-2 tests points within 24° (with nasal points to 30°).

17. Which drug produces the longest-lasting cycloplegia?

Show answer

Answer: Atropine. Atropine's effect lasts 1–2 weeks; tropicamide a few hours.

18. Phenylephrine dilates the pupil by acting on the:

Show answer

Answer: Dilator pupillae. It is an alpha-agonist stimulating the dilator muscle; it does not cause cycloplegia.

19. Before dilating an elderly patient's pupils, the technician should check for:

Show answer

Answer: A shallow anterior chamber. A shallow chamber (narrow angle) carries a risk of angle closure; inform the doctor.

20. A patient with a chemical splash in the eye should first:

Show answer

Answer: Have immediate copious irrigation. Immediate irrigation for at least 15–20 minutes is the most important step.

21. Sudden flashes with a curtain over vision suggests:

Show answer

Answer: Retinal detachment. This is a same-day emergency.

22. Which glaucoma drug class should be avoided in asthma?

Show answer

Answer: Beta-blockers. Topical beta-blockers such as timolol can cause bronchospasm.

23. The cap colour of prostaglandin analogue bottles in the AAO colour code is:

Show answer

Answer: Teal (turquoise). Yellow is for beta-blockers, red for mydriatics, pink for steroids.

24. Long-term topical steroid use can cause:

Show answer

Answer: Raised IOP and cataract. Steroid response glaucoma and posterior subcapsular cataract.

25. Ishihara plates mainly test:

Show answer

Answer: Red–green defects. Ishihara screens red–green (protan and deutan) defects.

26. The near point of accommodation increases (recedes) with age because of:

Show answer

Answer: Loss of lens elasticity. Presbyopia results from the lens becoming less elastic.

27. The keratometer measures:

Show answer

Answer: Anterior corneal curvature. Keratometry gives the corneal curvature (K readings) used in IOL calculation.

28. Specular microscopy is used to assess the:

Show answer

Answer: Corneal endothelium. It counts and photographs endothelial cells.

29. OCT is based on:

Show answer

Answer: Low-coherence interferometry with light. OCT uses light interferometry, giving micrometre-level images.

30. Fluorescein angiography carries a small risk of:

Show answer

Answer: Allergic reactions including anaphylaxis. Nausea is common; serious allergy is rare but resuscitation equipment must be ready.

31. The standard autoclave cycle for wrapped instruments is commonly:

Show answer

Answer: 121 °C for about 15–30 minutes or 134 °C for a few minutes. Exact times depend on the machine and load; follow the manufacturer's validated cycle.

32. Biomedical waste: used sharps and needles go in the:

Show answer

Answer: White puncture-proof container. India's BMW rules use a white (translucent) puncture-proof container for sharps.

33. Contaminated recyclable plastic waste such as tubing and gloves goes in the:

Show answer

Answer: Red bag. Red is for contaminated recyclable plastics; yellow for anatomical and soiled waste; blue for glassware.

34. A tonometer prism should be disinfected between patients mainly to prevent:

Show answer

Answer: Spread of adenovirus and other infections. Adenoviral conjunctivitis spreads easily through instruments.

35. An adult with a sudden painful red eye, haloes and vomiting most likely has:

Show answer

Answer: Acute angle-closure glaucoma. An ophthalmic emergency; inform the doctor immediately.

36. Amblyopia is best treated:

Show answer

Answer: In early childhood. Treatment works best while the visual system is developing.

37. A child's glasses prescription is most accurate when refraction is done:

Show answer

Answer: With cycloplegic drops. Children accommodate strongly; cycloplegia reveals the true error.

38. The blind spot on a visual field corresponds to the:

Show answer

Answer: Optic disc. The optic disc has no photoreceptors.

39. For legal purposes in India, blindness is currently defined (NPCBVI, 2017) as presenting visual acuity worse than:

Show answer

Answer: 3/60. India revised its definition to below 3/60 in the better eye (in line with WHO).

40. A patient with diabetes should have a dilated retinal check:

Show answer

Answer: At least once a year. Retinopathy is silent in early stages; yearly checks find it in time.
