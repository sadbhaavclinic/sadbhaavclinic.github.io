OptomDecoded Free Sample Mock 3: 50 Optometry Licensing Exam MCQs from the Book

For: Optometrists

Last updated: November 2026

A free 50-question optometry mock taken from OptomDecoded: Comprehensive Optometry, the licensing exam question bank by Dr Dhaval Patel. It uses the odd-numbered questions (1 to 99) of the book's Mock Test 3, so it samples the whole syllabus: optics, dispensing, contact lenses, binocular vision, ocular disease, pharmacology, low vision and practice.

  • Format: four options, single best answer, 50 minutes (about one minute per question, as in the book's full mocks).
  • Results by topic, so you can see which chapters to revisit.
  • After each answer: the explanation, why the other options are wrong, and a one-line pearl, exactly as in the book.
