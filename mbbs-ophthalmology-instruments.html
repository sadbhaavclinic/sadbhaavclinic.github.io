Ophthalmology Instruments for MBBS Practical Exams: How to Identify Them and What Examiners Ask

For: MBBS students

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • Name the instrument, say what it is used for, describe one identifying feature, and mention one operation where it is used.
  • Cataract set: wire speculum, toothed fixation forceps, crescent knife, keratome, cystitome, Sinskey hook, lens loop (vectis), Simcoe cannula, McPherson forceps.
  • Lacrimal set: Nettleship punctum dilator, Bowman probes, lacrimal cannula.
  • Chalazion set: chalazion clamp, scoop (curette), number 11 blade.
  • Schiotz tonometer is an indentation tonometer; the reading is converted to mmHg with a chart.

In most university practicals you are handed a tray of instruments. Examiners expect you to name each one, give its use, point out how you recognised it, and answer one or two follow-up questions. This page lists the usual instruments in that format.

MBBS syllabus (NMC CBME): OP7.4, OP7.5, OP2.3, OP6.7 · see the full syllabus map

How to answer

Four-line answer

  1. Name: "This is a wire speculum (universal eye speculum)."
  2. Identification: "It is made of a single spring wire with two loops that hold the lids."
  3. Uses: "To keep the lids apart during eye surgery and procedures."
  4. Extra point: "It is avoided in open globe injuries because it can press on the eye; a lid retractor is used instead."

Other common tray items

  • Enucleation scissors and spoon: enucleation (removing the eye), for retinoblastoma or painful blind eye.
  • Evisceration spoon: evisceration (removing the contents), for endophthalmitis.
  • Iris repositor: repositions iris.
  • Bard-Parker handle with No. 11 or 15 blade: skin and conjunctival incisions.

Exam pearls

  • Answer in four lines: name, feature, use, extra point.
  • Schiotz reading is converted with a table.
  • Direct ophthalmoscope: erect image, 15x.
  • Bowman probes for congenital NLDO.

Common mistakes

  • Calling a keratome a crescent knife.
  • Saying the speculum is used in open globe injury.
