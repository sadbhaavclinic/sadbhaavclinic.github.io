OCT Macula Reference Card: Normal Thickness, DME and Hole Staging

Last updated: October 2026

Clinic Desk ReferenceRetina

A one-screen checklist for macular OCT reports: is the scan trustworthy, which layer is involved, how thick is it compared with normal, and which disease-specific cut-offs apply?

Step 1: is the scan reliable?

  • Signal strength or quality index above the manufacturer's threshold (for example Cirrus signal strength 6/10 or more; Spectralis Q score about 20 dB or more).
  • Centred on the fovea, with no segmentation errors. Always look at the B-scans, not just the thickness map: misplaced lines produce false "thinning" or "thickening".
  • Compare with the previous scan using the same device and the follow-up (registration) mode.

Step 2: normal central subfield thickness (CST)

DeviceTypical normal CSTCentre-involved DME threshold (DRCR Retina Network)
Zeiss CirrusAbout 250–270 µmWomen 290 µm or more; men 305 µm or more
Heidelberg SpectralisAbout 270–300 µmWomen 305 µm or more; men 320 µm or more
Other devices (Topcon, Optovue and others)Use the device's own normative database. Thickness values are not interchangeable between machines, because they place the outer boundary at different layers.

Step 3: name the layer, then the disease

Finding and locationThink of
Intraretinal cysts (inner or outer nuclear layer)DME, RVO, uveitic and post-cataract CME (Irvine-Gass), wet AMD, retinitis pigmentosa, epiretinal membrane traction
Subretinal fluid, smooth domeCSCR (look for a thick choroid and RPE bumps), wet AMD, VKH (multiple loculated pockets with septae)
Sub-RPE elevationDrusen (dome, medium reflectivity), PED: serous (hollow), fibrovascular (filled), drusenoid; polypoidal lesions (sharp-peaked PED, "double-layer sign")
Hyper-reflective material above the RPEType 2 MNV, subretinal haemorrhage, vitelliform material, fibrosis
Outer retinal loss with choroidal hypertransmissionAtrophy: cRORA when 250 µm or more. See geographic atrophy.
Inner retinal hyper-reflectivity, then thinningAcute and old artery occlusion; PAMM (middle-layer band)
Ellipsoid zone disruption onlySolar or laser injury, MEWDS, AZOOR, early hydroxychloroquine toxicity (parafoveal "flying saucer" sign), MacTel

Step 4: vitreomacular interface

ConditionKey cut-offs (International Vitreomacular Traction Study, 2013)
Vitreomacular adhesion (VMA)Perifoveal vitreous separation with attachment within 3 mm of the fovea, normal foveal contour
Vitreomacular traction (VMT)As VMA, but with distortion of the fovea. Focal: 1500 µm or less; broad: over 1500 µm.
Full-thickness macular hole: size (minimum linear diameter)Small 250 µm or less; medium over 250 to 400 µm; large over 400 µm. Note whether VMT is present.
Lamellar hole vs macular pseudohole (2020 consensus)Lamellar hole: irregular foveal contour, foveal cavity with undermined edges and loss of foveal tissue (often epiretinal proliferation). Pseudohole: steep foveal walls from an ERM without tissue loss.
Epiretinal membrane (Govetto staging)Stage 1: foveal pit present. Stage 2: pit lost, layers identifiable. Stage 3: continuous ectopic inner foveal layers (EIFL). Stage 4: EIFL with disorganised layers. Higher stage means worse vision and outcome.

Step 5: disease-specific markers

  • DME biomarkers: DRIL (disorganised retinal inner layers), hyper-reflective foci, subretinal fluid, ellipsoid zone integrity and vitreomacular interface. See OCT biomarkers in DMO.
  • Wet AMD activity: new or increased intraretinal or subretinal fluid, a growing PED, or new haemorrhage. Persistent stable subretinal fluid can be tolerated in treat-and-extend regimens; intraretinal fluid is worse for vision.
  • CSCR: subretinal fluid with a thick choroid (pachychoroid) and dilated Haller vessels; check for a small PED at the leak point.
  • Hydroxychloroquine screening: look for parafoveal (or, in Asian patients, pericentral) loss of the ellipsoid zone. See the screening schedules card.

Common artefacts: segmentation errors in high myopia, ERM and large PEDs; mirror artefacts; decentration; and media opacity giving false thinning. If the number does not fit the picture, trust the B-scan.
