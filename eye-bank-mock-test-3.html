Eye Banking Mock Test 3: Retrieval, Evaluation, Storage, Quality and Management (40 MCQs)

For: Allied eye-care staff

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • 40 original questions on retrieval, evaluation, storage, quality and management.
  • Answer all, then press "Check my score": correct answers turn green, wrong ones red.
  • Open "Show answer" for the explanation.
  • Revise from the course modules.

Eye Banking course: Course home · M1 · M2 · M3 · M4 · M5 · M6 · M7 · M8 · M9 · M10

Practise: Mock 1 · Mock 2 · Mock 3 · Viva and OSCE · Career guide

Set a timer for 40 minutes. The option order is mixed, so read every choice.

1. Ideal retrieval timing is:

Show answer

Answer: Within about 6 hours of death. Earlier is better.

2. In-situ excision places the cornea:

Show answer

Answer: Directly into storage medium. Immediate preservation.

3. During enucleation, the cornea must be:

Show answer

Answer: Protected from pressure and touch. Endothelium.

4. After whole globe removal, the socket is:

Show answer

Answer: Filled with cotton or an eye cap and lids closed. Restoration.

5. Labels must be applied:

Show answer

Answer: Immediately at retrieval. Avoid mix-ups.

6. Blood for serology after death should be drawn:

Show answer

Answer: As soon as possible. Standards set limits.

7. Slit lamp evaluation of epithelium often shows:

Show answer

Answer: Defects and sloughing after death. Common.

8. Specular microscopy should count:

Show answer

Answer: An adequate number of cells in several areas. Reliable ECD.

9. Low ECD tissue is suitable for:

Show answer

Answer: Tectonic grafts or research. Allocation.

10. Tissue grading:

Show answer

Answer: Guides allocation to the right surgery. Matching.

11. Who reviews and releases tissue?

Show answer

Answer: Medical director. Accountability.

12. MK medium contains:

Show answer

Answer: TC-199, dextran and gentamicin. Composition.

13. Corneas in MK should be stored at:

Show answer

Answer: 2–8 °C. Refrigerated.

14. Organ culture needs a deswelling step because:

Show answer

Answer: Tissue swells in culture. Before surgery.

15. Freezing corneas in MK medium:

Show answer

Answer: Destroys endothelial cells. Ice crystals.

16. A refrigerator alarm indicates:

Show answer

Answer: Temperature out of range: act per SOP. Cold chain.

17. Sclera can be stored in:

Show answer

Answer: Alcohol or glycerol. Per standards.

18. Pre-cut DSAEK uses a:

Show answer

Answer: Microkeratome and artificial anterior chamber. Lamellar cut.

19. DMEK grafts are marked with an S-stamp to:

Show answer

Answer: Show correct orientation. Avoid upside-down grafts.

20. Traceability links:

Show answer

Answer: Donor to recipient. Safety.

21. When a recipient of one cornea develops infection, the mate cornea's surgeon is:

Show answer

Answer: Alerted. Investigation.

22. Primary graft failure should be:

Show answer

Answer: Reported and investigated. Quality.

23. An SOP is:

Show answer

Answer: A controlled written procedure. Consistency.

24. Laminar flow certification is:

Show answer

Answer: Done periodically. Asepsis.

25. Environmental monitoring uses:

Show answer

Answer: Settle plates or air sampling. Microbial.

26. Death-to-preservation time is improved by:

Show answer

Answer: Quick notification and 24-hour teams. Process.

27. Biomedical sharps go into:

Show answer

Answer: White puncture-proof container. Indian rules.

28. Staff exposure to donor blood requires:

Show answer

Answer: Post-exposure prophylaxis protocol. Safety.

29. Eye bank data on discard reasons help to:

Show answer

Answer: Reduce waste. Improvement.

30. Counsellor deployment should follow:

Show answer

Answer: Data on deaths and donations by hospital. Efficiency.

31. Processing charges for tissue must be:

Show answer

Answer: Transparent and lawful. Ethics.

32. Recognition events for donor families:

Show answer

Answer: Build community trust. Gratitude.

33. A CEBT holder is a:

Show answer

Answer: Certified eye bank technician (USA). EBAA credential.

34. Indian eye bank training is mainly through:

Show answer

Answer: Certificate courses at large eye hospitals/eye banks. Training.

35. Glycerol-preserved tissue is stored at:

Show answer

Answer: Room temperature (anhydrous). Long-term tectonic.

36. A cornea with stromal scar in the optical zone is best used for:

Show answer

Answer: Tectonic or research. Depends on location.

37. For DSAEK, the surgeon needs:

Show answer

Answer: A posterior lamellar lenticule with healthy endothelium. EK.

38. Chain-of-custody forms document:

Show answer

Answer: Who handled tissue and when. Traceability.

39. Temperature of transport boxes should be:

Show answer

Answer: Monitored (2–8 °C). Cold chain.

40. Tissue utilisation rate equals:

Show answer

Answer: Tissues transplanted ÷ tissues retrieved. Key KPI.
