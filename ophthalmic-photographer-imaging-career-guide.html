Ophthalmic Photographer and Imaging Technician: Fundus, OCT, Angiography, Certification (CRA, OCT-C) and Practice Questions

For: Allied eye-care staff

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • Imaging technicians produce the pictures and scans that drive decisions in diabetic retinopathy, AMD and glaucoma.
  • Skills: fundus and slit-lamp photography, OCT and OCT angiography, fluorescein and ICG angiography, widefield imaging, corneal imaging.
  • In India most imaging staff are optometrists or technicians trained on the job; in the USA the OPS offers the CRA and OCT-C credentials.
  • Good imaging also means good data management and patient confidentiality.

Careers in eye care: Careers in eye care (all roles) · Ophthalmic nurse · Orthoptist · Optician / dispensing optician · Ocularist · Eye bank technician · Ophthalmic photographer · Low vision and rehabilitation · Camps, scribes, counsellors, engineers · Ophthalmic technician

A sharp, well-centred image can save an injection, a laser session or a wrong decision. Ophthalmic imaging is a growing career as clinics adopt OCT, widefield cameras and teleophthalmology.

What imaging staff do

  • Colour, red-free and autofluorescence fundus photography; widefield imaging
  • OCT of macula and optic nerve; OCT angiography
  • Fluorescein and ICG angiography with the team (consent, cannula, allergy readiness)
  • Anterior segment and slit-lamp photography; corneal topography
  • Diabetic screening and teleophthalmology programmes
  • Image quality control, archiving and secure sharing

See fundus photography, OCT made easy and angiography practice.

Training and certification

WhereRoute
IndiaB.Optom or diploma in ophthalmic technology, then on-the-job training in imaging departments; manufacturer training courses
USAOPS Board of Certification: Certified Retinal Angiographer (CRA: experience, CPR, portfolio and written exam) and OCT-C (portfolio and written exam); IJCAHPO specialty credentials
UKHealthcare Science (Ophthalmic Imaging) degree apprenticeship; registration with the Academy for Healthcare Science

Skills checklist

  • Pupil dilation and patient positioning
  • Focus, exposure and centration; montage imaging
  • OCT scan protocols and artefact recognition
  • Angiography timing (early, mid, late phases)
  • Emergency readiness for allergic reactions
  • DICOM and secure image storage

Practice questions (interview and viva)

Tap a question to see a model answer.

What are the phases of a fluorescein angiogram?

Choroidal (pre-arterial), arterial, arteriovenous (capillary), venous and late (recirculation) phases.

Name common OCT artefacts.

Low signal, decentration, segmentation error, motion and blink artefacts, mirror artefact, media opacity.

What must be ready before FFA?

Consent, allergy history, intravenous access, and an emergency kit with adrenaline and a doctor available.

Why is red-free photography useful?

It highlights the nerve fibre layer, haemorrhages and blood vessels.

What is the CRA?

Certified Retinal Angiographer, the main certification of the Ophthalmic Photographers’ Society Board of Certification.
