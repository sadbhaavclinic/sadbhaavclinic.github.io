Eye Care Support Roles Study Course: Screening, Camps, Counselling, Scribing, Safety and Equipment

For: Optometrists

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • A full course for the people who keep eye services running: screeners, camp teams, counsellors, scribes, front-office staff and equipment engineers.
  • Written for Indian practice (national programme, biomedical waste rules, insurance pre-authorisation), with international roles for comparison.
  • Each module is practical: checklists, scripts and red flags.
  • 10 module notes pages, 3 mock tests and a viva and OSCE page: 190 MCQs in all.

Eye hospitals and clinics depend on many roles besides doctors and optometrists. This course gives support staff the knowledge to screen accurately, counsel honestly, document safely, keep the clinic clean and running, and look after equipment.

Training in India is mostly in-house at eye hospitals, with certificate courses and skill council qualifications for vision technicians; in the USA, the American Society of Ophthalmic Administrators offers the Certified Ophthalmic Executive credential. See the support roles career guide.

Recommended references

  • National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment: operational guidelines (vision centres, outreach, school eye health)
  • WHO: Primary Eye Care Training Manual; Hand Hygiene in Health Care
  • Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016 (India)
  • NABH accreditation standards for hospitals (equipment and quality chapters)
  • American Society of Ophthalmic Administrators: practice management resources
  • IEC 62353 (recurrent testing of medical electrical equipment); IEC 60825 (laser safety)

Books are listed as study references only; use the latest editions and your course’s prescribed texts.

  • Camp days: 5
  • Counselling sessions: 50
  • Scribed consultations: 100
  • Telephone triage calls (supervised): 50
  • Equipment daily checks logged: 60
  • BLS course completed
  • Infection control audit participation: 2
