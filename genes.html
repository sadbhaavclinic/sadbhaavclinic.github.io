Coats disease is not inherited; somatic NDP mutations have been found in some eyes. Thiel–Behnke dystrophy was first mapped to chromosome 10 but is caused by TGFBI on chromosome 5.

Where residents slip

  • Putting both Meesmann genes on one chromosome: KRT3 is on 12, KRT12 on 17.
  • Calling LHON X-linked because men are affected more: it is mitochondrial (maternal).
  • Forgetting WAGR: sporadic aniridia needs screening for Wilms tumour.
