Welcome to RPC Rounds

RPC Rounds is a free ophthalmology teaching site for residents, fellows, optometrists and students. I started it as a resident at the Dr. Rajendra Prasad Centre for Ophthalmic Sciences (RPC), AIIMS, New Delhi, to share notes with fellow residents, first on rpcround.wordpress.com and now here.

Today it has more than 480 pages covering the whole syllabus, organised by subspecialty, with a 189-topic revision checklist, 123 viva and OSCE stations, free timed mock tests, a separate track for optometrists, a section for MBBS students mapped to the CBME curriculum, daily flashcards and a clinic desk reference. Every page is written and medically reviewed by me, based on standard textbooks, landmark trials and current guidelines, with sources listed on each page. Read how content is written and checked in our editorial policy, or read about RPC Rounds (including how to cite and share it).
