RPC Rounds for Medical Students: Ophthalmology for MBBS, Mapped to the CBME Curriculum

For: MBBS students

Last updated: November 2026

RPC Rounds for Medical Students is a free ophthalmology course for MBBS students and interns, mapped to the National Medical Commission's competency-based curriculum (CBME). Short, clear pages cover what you need for the posting, the university theory and practical exams, and the ophthalmology part of NEET-PG, INI-CET, FMGE and NExT. Each page shows its CBME codes, exam pearls, flip cards and a quiz, and links to the deeper resident page when you want more.

Every day: a new clinical case in Today in the Eye OPD.

Start with: the eye examination routine and red eye. Then test yourself with three free student mocks (core concepts, clinical vignettes, practical exam). Revise on the go with the student Anki deck.

A 4-week posting plan

WeekReadDo in the OPD and theatre
Week 1Examination routine, visual acuity, red eye, conjunctiva and corneaExamine five patients fully; evert ten lids; stain one corneal ulcer
Week 2Cataract, glaucoma, refractive errors, lids and lacrimalWatch cataract surgery; do pinhole and digital tonometry; check ROPLAS
Week 3Retina, sudden loss of vision, neuro-ophthalmology, squint and childrenPractise distant direct ophthalmoscopy; swinging torch test; Hirschberg and cover test
Week 4Systemic disease, drugs, injuries, community ophthalmology; revisionWrite two case sheets; instruments and spotters; take all three mocks

Exams after MBBS

  • NEET-PG and INI-CET: ophthalmology is a short subject with many easy marks; one-liners on signs, drugs, eponyms and community ophthalmology recur. Practise with the NEET-PG mock.
  • FMGE and NExT: clinical vignettes and first-step management; see mock 2.
  • Going further: if ophthalmology becomes your specialty, continue with the First 6 Months Track and the resident blog.
