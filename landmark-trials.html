Landmark Trials in Ophthalmology: High-Yield Summary

Last updated: October 2026

Landmark trials are the backbone of evidence-based ophthalmology and a favourite of every viva examiner. This page condenses the trials residents are most often asked about into one line each: the question the trial asked, and the take-home message you need to remember.

How to use this page: for each trial, learn the question and the answer first. Numbers are given only where they are classic and frequently examined. For details, always go back to the original publication.

Glaucoma

TrialQuestionTake-home message
OHTS
Ocular Hypertension Treatment Study		Does lowering IOP in ocular hypertension prevent glaucoma?Lowering IOP by about 20% reduced 5-year conversion to POAG from 9.5% to 4.4%. Thin central corneal thickness emerged as an independent risk factor.
EMGT
Early Manifest Glaucoma Trial		Does treating early glaucoma slow progression compared with no treatment?Treatment (laser trabeculoplasty plus betaxolol) reduced progression (45% vs 62%). Each 1 mmHg of IOP lowering reduced progression risk by about 10%.
CNTGS
Collaborative Normal-Tension Glaucoma Study		Does lowering IOP help normal-tension glaucoma?A 30% IOP reduction slowed progression, but many untreated eyes did not progress over 5 years. Its methodology has recently been questioned — see our NTG page.
AGIS
Advanced Glaucoma Intervention Study		Which surgical sequence works best in advanced glaucoma, and what IOP is safe?Eyes whose IOP stayed below 18 mmHg at every visit showed almost no average visual field progression. Lower and more consistent IOP matters in advanced disease.
CIGTS
Collaborative Initial Glaucoma Treatment Study		Medicines or trabeculectomy as initial treatment for newly diagnosed glaucoma?Visual field outcomes were similar. Surgery achieved lower IOP but caused more cataract.
TVT
Tube Versus Trabeculectomy Study		Tube or trabeculectomy with MMC in eyes with previous cataract or failed glaucoma surgery?The tube (Baerveldt 350) had a higher success rate over 5 years, with similar final IOP.
LiGHT
Laser in Glaucoma and Ocular Hypertension Trial		SLT first or drops first?First-line SLT was at least as good as drops, was cost-effective, and about three-quarters of SLT eyes were drop-free at 3 years.
EAGLEClear lens extraction or laser iridotomy in primary angle closure?In PACG, and in PAC with IOP ≥30 mmHg, clear lens extraction gave better IOP control and quality of life and was cost-effective.
ZAP
Zhongshan Angle Closure Prevention trial		Should every primary angle-closure suspect get a prophylactic iridotomy?Iridotomy reduced the risk of progression, but the absolute risk was so low that routine widespread prophylactic LPI was not recommended.

Diabetic retinopathy and macular oedema

TrialQuestionTake-home message
DRS
Diabetic Retinopathy Study		Does panretinal photocoagulation prevent vision loss in PDR?PRP reduced severe vision loss by at least 50%, with the greatest benefit in eyes with high-risk characteristics.
ETDRS
Early Treatment Diabetic Retinopathy Study		Focal laser for macular oedema? Early PRP? Aspirin?Focal laser for clinically significant macular oedema (CSME) halved moderate vision loss. Early PRP is not advised for mild to moderate NPDR. Aspirin neither helped nor harmed retinopathy or vitreous haemorrhage. ETDRS also defined CSME.
DRVS
Diabetic Retinopathy Vitrectomy Study		Early vitrectomy for severe vitreous haemorrhage?Early vitrectomy benefited type 1 diabetics.
DRCR Protocol TAflibercept vs bevacizumab vs ranibizumab for centre-involving DME?All three were similar when starting vision was 20/32 to 20/40. With vision of 20/50 or worse, aflibercept gave better vision at 1 year.
DRCR Protocol SAnti-VEGF or PRP for proliferative diabetic retinopathy?Ranibizumab was non-inferior to PRP at 2 years, with less peripheral field loss and less DME. Its weakness: patients who miss follow-up are at risk.
DRCR Protocol VDoes centre-involving DME with good vision (20/25 or better) need treatment immediately?Observation (treating only if vision worsened), laser, and immediate aflibercept gave similar vision at 2 years.

AMD and vein occlusions

TrialQuestionTake-home message
AREDS / AREDS2Can antioxidant supplements slow AMD?Supplements reduced progression to advanced AMD by about 25% in intermediate AMD or advanced AMD in one eye. AREDS2 replaced beta-carotene with lutein and zeaxanthin because of the lung cancer risk in smokers.
MARINA / ANCHORDoes monthly ranibizumab work for neovascular AMD?Ranibizumab improved vision, compared with sham (MARINA) and with verteporfin PDT (ANCHOR). This began the anti-VEGF era.
CATT / IVANIs bevacizumab as good as ranibizumab?Bevacizumab gave equivalent visual outcomes when used on the same dosing schedule.
BVOS
Branch Vein Occlusion Study		Laser for BRVO?Grid laser improved vision in macular oedema with VA 20/40 or worse. Scatter laser reduced vitreous haemorrhage once neovascularisation developed.
CVOS
Central Vein Occlusion Study		Laser for CRVO?Grid laser did not improve vision in CRVO macular oedema. Prophylactic PRP is not recommended; treat promptly once neovascularisation appears (2 clock hours of iris NV or any angle NV).
SCOREIntravitreal triamcinolone for vein-occlusion oedema?In CRVO, triamcinolone 1 mg was better than observation. In BRVO, it was no better than grid laser and had more side effects.
BRAVO / CRUISERanibizumab for vein-occlusion oedema?Ranibizumab gave rapid and significant visual gain in both BRVO and CRVO.

Vitreoretina, endophthalmitis and ROP

TrialQuestionTake-home message
EVS
Endophthalmitis Vitrectomy Study		Immediate vitrectomy or tap-and-inject after cataract surgery? Systemic antibiotics?Immediate vitrectomy helped only when vision was light perception. With hand movements or better, tap-and-inject was enough. Systemic antibiotics added no benefit.
Silicone StudyWhich tamponade for PVR?Silicone oil and C3F8 gas were similar to each other and both better than SF6.
CRYO-ROPDoes cryotherapy help threshold ROP?Cryotherapy reduced unfavourable structural outcomes.
ETROPShould we treat before threshold?Treat type 1 (high-risk prethreshold) ROP; observe type 2.
BEAT-ROPIntravitreal bevacizumab or laser?Bevacizumab reduced recurrence in zone I stage 3+ disease, but not in zone II. Late reactivation and long-term systemic safety need follow-up.

Cornea, infection and cataract

TrialQuestionTake-home message
HEDS
Herpetic Eye Disease Study		What helps herpes simplex keratitis and iritis?Topical steroids (with antiviral cover) reduced persistence and progression of stromal keratitis. Long-term oral acyclovir 400 mg twice daily significantly reduced recurrences of ocular HSV.
SCUT
Steroids for Corneal Ulcers Trial		Do adjunctive topical steroids help bacterial keratitis?No overall difference in vision at 3 months. Some severe, non-Nocardia ulcers may benefit.
MUTT INatamycin or voriconazole for filamentous fungal keratitis?Natamycin was better, especially for Fusarium.
MUTT IIDoes adding oral voriconazole help severe fungal ulcers?No overall benefit.
ESCRS Endophthalmitis StudyDoes intracameral cefuroxime prevent post-cataract endophthalmitis?Intracameral cefuroxime reduced endophthalmitis about fivefold.

Neuro-ophthalmology

TrialQuestionTake-home message
ONTT
Optic Neuritis Treatment Trial		Should optic neuritis be treated with steroids?IV methylprednisolone sped recovery but did not change final vision. Oral prednisone alone (standard dose) increased recurrences and should be avoided. Brain MRI lesions were the strongest predictor of MS: the 15-year risk was about 25% with a normal MRI and about 72% with one or more lesions.
IONDT
Ischemic Optic Neuropathy Decompression Trial		Does optic nerve sheath decompression help NAION?No benefit, and possibly harm. Abandon it.
IIHTT
Idiopathic Intracranial Hypertension Treatment Trial		Does acetazolamide help mild visual loss in IIH?Acetazolamide plus diet gave a modest improvement in visual field, papilloedema and quality of life compared with diet alone.

Uvea and ocular oncology

TrialQuestionTake-home message
COMS
Collaborative Ocular Melanoma Study		Plaque brachytherapy or enucleation for choroidal melanoma?For medium tumours, survival was the same with either. For large tumours, radiation before enucleation added no survival benefit. See our COMS page.
MUST
Multicenter Uveitis Steroid Treatment trial		Fluocinolone implant or systemic therapy for non-infectious intermediate, posterior and panuveitis?Both worked. The implant caused more cataract and glaucoma, and long-term follow-up favoured systemic therapy for vision.

Paediatrics and strabismus

TrialQuestionTake-home message
PEDIG ATS
Amblyopia Treatment Studies		How much patching or atropine is needed?For moderate amblyopia, 2 hours of daily patching was as effective as 6 hours. Weekend atropine was as effective as daily atropine. Patching and atropine gave similar results. See our amblyopia page.
IATS
Infant Aphakia Treatment Study		Primary IOL or contact lens after unilateral infant cataract surgery (1–6 months of age)?Visual outcomes were similar, but the IOL group had more adverse events and reoperations. Contact lens aphakia remains reasonable in young infants.
LAMP
Low-Concentration Atropine for Myopia Progression		Which low-dose atropine concentration controls myopia best?Of 0.01%, 0.025% and 0.05%, the 0.05% concentration was the most effective and still well tolerated.
CITT
Convergence Insufficiency Treatment Trial		Which treatment works for convergence insufficiency?Office-based vergence/accommodative therapy with home reinforcement was the most effective.

Viva tip: examiners rarely want the exact percentages. They want the clinical decision the trial changed. Always end your answer with "…so in practice, we now …".

This is a revision summary. Before quoting figures in a paper or presentation, please verify them against the primary publications.
