Ophthalmic Dispensing Mock Test 3: Multifocals, Frames, Verification, Fitting and Practice (41 MCQs)

For: Allied eye-care staff

Last updated: November 2026

Key points in 60 seconds

  • 41 original questions on multifocals, frames, verification, fitting and practice.
  • Answer all, then press "Check my score": correct answers turn green, wrong ones red.
  • Open "Show answer" for the explanation.
  • Revise from the course modules.

Ophthalmic Dispensing course: Course home · M1 · M2 · M3 · M4 · M5 · M6 · M7 · M8 · M9 · M10

Practise: Mock 1 · Mock 2 · Mock 3 · Viva and OSCE · Career guide

Set a timer for 41 minutes. The option order is mixed, so read every choice.

1. The top of a standard adult bifocal segment is usually set at the:

Show answer

Answer: Lower lid margin. Adjust for tasks.

2. Segment inset per eye is usually about:

Show answer

Answer: 2–2.5 mm. Near PD.

3. Image jump in a 28 mm D-segment with OC 5 mm below the top and add +2.00 is:

Show answer

Answer: 1Δ. 0.5 cm × 2.

4. Which progressive complaint suggests fitting cross too low?

Show answer

Answer: Tilting head back to read. Near zone too low.

5. Minimum fitting height depends on:

Show answer

Answer: The progressive design's corridor. Short vs long corridors.

6. Progressive engravings include the add and:

Show answer

Answer: Brand/design identifier. For re-marking.

7. Before measuring PAL heights, the frame must be:

Show answer

Answer: Adjusted to the face. Accurate heights.

8. Degressive lenses are designed for:

Show answer

Answer: Desk and computer work. Near-variable.

9. Boxing system A is:

Show answer

Answer: Horizontal box length. Lens width.

10. Frame marked 50□20 has box centre distance of:

Show answer

Answer: 70 mm. A + DBL.

11. Which frame material is most hypoallergenic and light?

Show answer

Answer: Titanium. Also stainless steel.

12. Acetate frames are adjusted by:

Show answer

Answer: Gentle heating. Frame warmer.

13. Near PD is typically less than distance PD by about:

Show answer

Answer: 3–4 mm. Convergence.

14. A focimeter measures distance power with the lens:

Show answer

Answer: Back surface against stop. Back vertex.

15. Front-surface bifocal adds are measured:

Show answer

Answer: With front surface on stop. Front vertex.

16. Prism thinning appears as:

Show answer

Answer: Equal base-down in both lenses. Normal.

17. The standard for mounted lens tolerances is:

Show answer

Answer: ISO 21987. Tolerances.

18. Axis errors matter most when:

Show answer

Answer: Cylinder is high. Residual cylinder.

19. Lashes touching lenses: adjust by:

Show answer

Answer: Increasing vertex distance. Move lens forward.

20. Frame higher on the left side: bend the:

Show answer

Answer: Left side down at the joint (or right side up). Level the front.

21. A new pair of plus lenses with a flatter base curve may cause:

Show answer

Answer: Altered magnification and distortion complaints. Non-tolerance.

22. New unwanted diplopia with new specs suggests checking:

Show answer

Answer: Centration and induced prism. Decentration.

23. For a child's first glasses, the most important fitting point is:

Show answer

Answer: Bridge fit. Weight on nose.

24. Bifocals for accommodative esotropia are set:

Show answer

Answer: High (bisecting the pupil). Child uses segment.

25. Contact lens wearers should not:

Show answer

Answer: Use tap water on lenses or sleep in them unless prescribed. Keratitis risk.

26. A patient reading N16 who needs N8 requires magnification of:

Show answer

Answer: 2×. 16/8.

27. Equivalent power for 3× magnification (M = F/4) is:

Show answer

Answer: +12 D. 4 × 3.

28. A sports frame with high wrap requires:

Show answer

Answer: Compensated power and prism (free-form). Avoid distortion.

29. A customer with sudden flashes and floaters asks for new glasses. You should:

Show answer

Answer: Advise same-day eye examination. Retinal tear risk.

30. A dispensing optician in the UK must be registered with the:

Show answer

Answer: GOC. General Optical Council.

31. The NCAHP Act, 2021 in India covers:

Show answer

Answer: Allied and healthcare professions including optometry. National Commission.

32. Honest advice about blue-filter lenses means:

Show answer

Answer: Saying evidence does not show benefit for eye strain or disease. Evidence-based.

33. A complaint about new glasses should first be met by:

Show answer

Answer: Listening, checking the glasses and fit, then solving. Systematic.

34. Rimless frames need lenses made of:

Show answer

Answer: Trivex or polycarbonate preferably. Drill strength.

35. Pantoscopic tilt affects:

Show answer

Answer: Effective power and OC height. Lower OCs 1 mm per 2°.

36. An executive bifocal suits:

Show answer

Answer: Accountants with wide near tasks. Wide segment.

37. When verifying progressives, prism is read at the:

Show answer

Answer: Prism reference point. Usually 4 mm below the fitting cross.

38. Photochromic lenses tested by a UV lamp should:

Show answer

Answer: Darken. Activation check.

39. Records of dispensing should include:

Show answer

Answer: Rx, measurements, order, verification and advice. Professional.

40. Safety spectacles must:

Show answer

Answer: Carry markings showing the standard met. Certification.

41. Polycarbonate lenses should not be cleaned with:

Show answer

Answer: Acetone or strong solvents. Damage.
