What to send for your free review

For eye problems

  • Your main problem and how long you have had it
  • Your current glasses prescription and vision, if known
  • Any eye reports: OCT scans, topography, biometry, visual fields, photos of the eye
  • Medical conditions (diabetes, blood pressure) and the medicines you take

For dental problems

  • A panoramic X-ray (OPG); for implants, a CBCT scan if you have one
  • Clear photos of your teeth: front, both sides, upper and lower
  • What you would like to change, and any dental work you already have
  • Medical conditions and the medicines you take

Please also tell us your country and when you would like to travel. Reports in English are easiest to review. If yours are in another language, send them anyway and we will tell you if a translation is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the report review really free?

Yes. Send your reports on WhatsApp or by email and our doctor will reply with a suggested plan and an estimated cost. There is no charge and no obligation.

Do you arrange visas, airport pickup or hotels?

No. You arrange your own visa, flights and accommodation. Our travel guide explains the e-Medical visa, typical flight routes and the areas close to the clinic.

How long will I need to stay in Ahmedabad?

It depends on the treatment. Most eye surgeries need about one week; some dental work needs one to two weeks, and dental implants usually need two trips a few months apart. We tell you the expected number of days in your free review.

Will the final cost be the same as the estimate?

The estimate is based on your reports. We examine you when you arrive and confirm the plan and the cost before any treatment begins. If the examination changes the plan, we explain why and give you the new cost first.

Can a family member come with me?

Yes. India offers an e-Medical Attendant visa for family members travelling with a patient on an e-Medical visa. See the travel guide for details.

What happens after I go home?

You leave with a written summary of your treatment and medicines. You can send follow-up questions on WhatsApp or by email, and we recommend regular check-ups with an eye or dental doctor in your home country.
